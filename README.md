One satirical sentence, roasted to order: a lawmaker drafting the bill to
police the internet, who still emails his staff a screenshot of a Word
document and asks them to turn it into a PDF.

## The entry

> The lawmaker drafting the bill to police the internet still emails his staff a screenshot of a Word document and asks them to make it a PDF.

140 characters, one sentence, punch on the last word.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when the harness sets it), with a
hard guard that refuses anything over the 320-character gate.

```sh
node main.ts
```
